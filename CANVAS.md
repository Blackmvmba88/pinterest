## Problem

Creative taste is spread across Pinterest, downloads, prompts, covers, music platforms, local folders, and project repositories.

The system needs to preserve and learn from these signals instead of letting them disappear inside temporary platform feeds.

## Solution

A personal creative intelligence system that collects, stores, analyzes, and scores visual and creative inspiration.
